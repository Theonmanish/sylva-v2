**Structure notes**

- Sections 01 to 05 carry very little text on purpose. The imagery does the work. Copy appears as short statements that arrive with the scroll, not as blocks to read.
- The tagline appears in section 01 and in the footer only.
- Every heading on the page is different, and no two sections repeat the same message. The vessel-led approach is stated in section 06, shown in section 09, and not repeated elsewhere.
- Each section from 06 onward has one primary CTA.

# Brand experience

## Section 06: Brand introduction

### Website copy

**Eyebrow:** Sylva Auris
**Main heading:** Terrariums and kokedama, from Moodbidri.

**Body:**

Sylva Auris creates terrariums and kokedama in Moodbidri, Karnataka.

A terrarium brings living plants, moss and natural hardscape, such as roots and stone, together within glass. The shape of the vessel decides how they are arranged: where a root rises, how moss spreads across the surface, which plants have room to grow.

Kokedama, a separate offering, presents a living plant through a moss-based planting technique.

**CTA label:** Explore the collection
**CTA destination:** /collection

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Explain what Sylva Auris creates, in plain terms.
- **Visual:** One large, real terrarium photograph in an asymmetric image and text layout. Use authentic product photography.
- **Derivation:** Adapted from the brand narrative in 01-brand-messaging.md (section 13.1). The line "Every composition begins with the vessel" was not used, because kokedama has no vessel. The text now refers to terrariums only for the vessel-led approach.
- **Terminology:** *Hardscape* is defined at first use, as required by the tone guide.
- **Not included here:** Care, customisation and philosophy appear in later sections.

## Section 08: Philosophy

### Website copy

**Eyebrow:** Philosophy
**Main heading:** A closer view of nature.

**Body:**

The idea behind Sylva Auris is simple: a person can observe the character of a forest in a much smaller setting.

In a terrarium, the vessel, hardscape, substrate, plants and moss all contribute, and each decision is made in relation to the others. The aim is a coherent landscape that rewards looking closely, not a container filled as full as possible.

**CTA label:** Read the philosophy
**CTA destination:** /philosophy

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Preview the thinking behind the brand and send visitors to the Philosophy page.
- **Visual:** Minimal. One restrained product image or none. Generous spacing.
- **Accuracy:** The closing sentence restates the confirmed design principle: a coherent composition, rather than fitting as many plants or decorative items as possible into a container.
- **The name:** The meaning of "Sylva Auris" is deliberately left to the Philosophy page, where it can be explained with the care noted in 01-brand-messaging.md (section 15.1). Do not add an explanation of the name to the homepage until the wording is confirmed. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]
- **Claims check:** No wellbeing, escape or tranquillity language. The experience is described as looking closely.

## Section 09: Atelier

### Website copy

**Eyebrow:** Atelier
**Main heading:** How a composition is made

**Body:**

The process starts with the vessel and works inward. Hardscape such as roots, wood and stone sets height and direction. Substrate forms the growing medium. Plants and moss are selected to suit the space and each other, then arranged as one landscape.

Custom compositions can be discussed around your preferred size, budget and setting. Plants, hardscape and gravel can be considered together, and vessel options discussed, subject to availability.

**CTA label:** Discover the Atelier
**CTA destination:** /atelier

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Show that a terrarium involves a sequence of decisions, and introduce custom work.
- **Visual:** Close-up photography of materials and the making process: substrate layers, roots, moss, plants. Real photography only.
- **Accuracy:**
  - Roots, wood, stone, substrate, plants and moss are confirmed elements.
  - Customisation currently discussed: plants, hardscape and gravel. Vessel options are discussed subject to availability.
  - No specific vessel, plant or material is promised.
- **Not stated, deliberately:** that materials are sourced after an order. This is an internal operating constraint, not customer copy. Do not describe any stock of materials.
- **Do not use** "bespoke", "handcrafted" or "artisanal" here. They are held back until the business confirms they describe the process. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]
- **"Atelier" label:** The page name does not imply a studio open to visitors. Do not add wording about studio visits or workshops.
- **Detail lives elsewhere:** The Atelier page (06-atelier-copy.md) expands each stage. Do not copy this paragraph there.

**Eyebrow:** Care
**Main heading:** Caring for a living composition

**Body:**

Terrariums are designed with low maintenance in mind. They are still living arrangements, and what they need depends on the plants, the vessel and where the piece is placed.

Kokedama has different needs, so its care is explained separately. The care guide covers light, watering, moisture and placement for both.

**CTA label:** Read the care guide
**CTA destination:** /care

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Set honest expectations and send visitors to the care guide.
- **Section title:** The planning label is "Care and Longevity". The visible heading uses "Care" only, as recommended in 01-brand-messaging.md (section 15.3). The business cannot guarantee how long a terrarium will last.
- **Claims check:** No "maintenance-free", "self-sustaining", "thrives" or lifespan wording. No watering schedule appears here. No schedule may be added unless the business verifies it.
- **Accuracy:** The care guide's scope (light, watering, moisture, placement) matches the Care page outline in the structure blueprint. The page content itself still requires confirmation. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION: care instructions for each product type, see 08-care-content.md]
