## Collection page overview [INTERNAL]

**Purpose of the page.** Make the two product categories easy to understand and explore, and give each piece a clear route to an enquiry. The page is not a store. There is no cart, checkout, shipping or stock status.

**Route:** /collection

| # | Section | Heading | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Collection introduction | Collection | None (scroll) |
| 02 | Terrariums | Terrariums | Enquire about this piece (per product) |
| 03 | Kokedama | Kokedama | Enquire about this piece (per product) |
| 04 | Custom compositions | Custom compositions | Enquire about a custom piece |
| 05 | Closing enquiry | Start with an enquiry | Make an enquiry |

**Structure notes**

- Terrariums and kokedama are presented as two distinct categories with separate copy, separate care links and no shared vocabulary beyond what both genuinely share (living plants, moss).
- The homepage already introduced the two categories and the vessel-led approach (document 02, sections 06 and 07). This page adds what the homepage does not: how pieces are presented, how they differ, where to find care guidance and how to enquire. It does not repeat the homepage paragraphs.
- Pieces are shown in large editorial presentations, not a grid of ecommerce cards.
- Each section has one primary CTA.

**Decisions recommended for this page**

1. **No filters or sorting.** The catalogue is small and not yet confirmed. Filters would suggest a larger range than exists and add interface the business cannot maintain. Revisit if the catalogue grows.
2. **No stock or availability labels.** There is no reliable system to keep them accurate, and the brand profile says to use such labels only if the business can keep them updated.
3. **Price: show all or none.** If prices are confirmed for every piece, show them. If only some are confirmed, show none and let the enquiry handle price. Mixed display weakens the premium impression. A "Price on enquiry" label is possible but should only be used if the business chooses it. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]

## Section 02: Terrariums

### Website copy

**Eyebrow:** Terrariums
**Main heading:** Terrariums
**Supporting heading:** Living plants and natural hardscape, composed within glass

**Body:**

Terrariums are the centre of the collection. The vessel shapes the composition, so the arrangement varies with its form and size. Where a piece is listed, the plants and materials it contains are named.

Terrariums are designed with low maintenance in mind. They are living arrangements and still need appropriate care.

**Care link label:** Read terrarium care
**Care link destination:** /care#terrarium-care

**Piece presentation (for each terrarium shown):**

**Per-piece link:** View this piece → /collection/[piece] (only if individual product pages are built)
**Per-piece CTA label:** Enquire about this piece
**Per-piece CTA destination:** /contact, with the enquiry type "Ready-made products" and the piece pre-selected

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Introduce the category and explain how pieces are presented, without repeating the homepage description.
- **Format versus size:** The brand has used two separate label sets: formats (Micro, Jar, Bowl, Cube) and size categories (Micro, Medium, Large). They describe different things. Do not use either set in copy until the final catalogue structure is confirmed, and never treat them as interchangeable. The body text speaks only of "form and size". [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION: catalogue structure]
- **Formats under consideration** (jar, bowl-in-jar, other vessel-led compositions) are "used or considered", not confirmed as current. Name only formats the business has confirmed.
- **Do not invent** dimensions, volumes, plant counts or plant species. Size categories differ mainly by vessel size and have no confirmed measurements.
- **Plants and materials:** Use the working vocabulary from the brand profile only when the specific piece contains them. Never copy a generic plant list onto every piece.
- **Care claims:** No "maintenance-free", "self-sustaining" or lifespan wording.
- **Layout:** Large product presentations. Alternating image and text. No borders, badges or decorative panels.
- **If individual product pages are not built:** Drop the "View this piece" link and show all details in the presentation itself. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION: whether product pages are required]

**Main heading:** Kokedama
**Supporting heading:** A separate offering

**Body:**

Kokedama presents a living plant through a moss-based planting technique. It has its own character and its own care, and is described and cared for separately from terrariums.

**Per-piece CTA label:** Enquire about this piece
**Per-piece CTA destination:** /contact, with the enquiry type "Kokedama" and the piece pre-selected

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Introduce kokedama on its own terms, as required by the brand profile.
- **Vocabulary rule:** Do not use *vessel*, *glass*, *hardscape* or *landscape* here. They belong to terrariums. Describe kokedama directly, in its own terms.
- **Technique:** The brand documents describe kokedama only as a moss-based planting technique. They do not specify materials, binding method, display style, plant range or sizes. Do not add any of these. The optional sentence is a general description of the technique and is held back until confirmed.
- **Meaning of the word:** *Kokedama* is a Japanese term. The brand documents do not discuss its origin, so the copy does not either. It can be added to the Philosophy or Atelier pages if the business wants it. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]
- **Care:** Terrarium care guidance must never be applied to kokedama. The care link goes to the kokedama section only.
- **Layout:** Same editorial treatment as terrariums, so the two categories feel equal. Do not present kokedama as an add-on.

## Section 04: Custom compositions

### Website copy

**Eyebrow:** Custom work
**Main heading:** Custom compositions
**Supporting heading:** Made around your space and occasion

**Body:**

A custom composition begins with a conversation. Share the size, budget, occasion and setting you have in mind. If this is your first terrarium, say so, as it helps us explain what to expect.

Plants, hardscape and gravel can then be considered together, and vessel options discussed, subject to availability.

**Primary CTA label:** Enquire about a custom piece
**Primary CTA destination:** /contact, with the enquiry type "Custom terrariums" pre-selected

**Secondary link label:** See how a composition is made
**Secondary link destination:** /atelier

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Introduce custom work briefly and send interested visitors to the enquiry. The full process sits on the Atelier page.
- **Accuracy:**
  - The enquiry topics (size, budget, occasion, setting, experience with terrariums) match the brand profile.
  - Currently discussed customisation: plants, hardscape and gravel. Vessel options are subject to availability.
  - Future ambitions (more glass quality options, more vessel sizes and shapes) are not mentioned. They are not current offers.
- **Not stated:** That materials are sourced after an order. This is an internal operating constraint and is not customer copy. Do not promise any material, vessel or plant before availability and cost are checked.
- **Do not use:** "Bespoke", "one-of-a-kind", "your imagination is our only limit", "unlimited options", "tailor-made luxury".
- **Instant configuration:** Nothing here suggests an online builder. The route is an enquiry.
- **Visual:** Close-up photography of materials or the making process, or a completed custom piece if one exists and the customer has agreed to its use. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]
- **Differences from the homepage:** Section 09 of the homepage already describes the process and custom work. This section adds the first-time-owner note and the direct enquiry route, and does not restate the process stages.
