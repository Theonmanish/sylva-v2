## Gallery page overview [INTERNAL]

**Purpose of the page.** Show actual Sylva Auris work so that visitors can judge the quality and visual style of the compositions. The gallery is a visual portfolio, not a decorative image collection and not a shop. It carries very little text.

**Route:** /gallery

| # | Section | Heading | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Introduction | Gallery | Explore the collection (link) |
| 02 | Main gallery | none (category labels only) | None |
| 03 | Featured work | Featured work | None |
| 04 | Custom work | Custom work | None |
| 05 | Closing enquiry | Ask about a custom composition | Enquire about a custom piece |

**Structure notes**

- **Photography-first.** Images carry the page. Text is limited to the introduction, category labels, short captions and the closing section.
- **Real photographs only.** Every image must be a photograph of an actual Sylva Auris piece. Generated, illustrative, stock or composited images must not appear, and must not be presented as available products. The five supplied cinematic homepage images are not gallery material unless each is confirmed as a real photograph of a real piece. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]
- **Categories follow the sitemap, not the earlier planning labels.** The blueprint suggests Jar, Bowl, Cube, Kokedama and Custom compositions. Those format labels are unconfirmed (see documents 03 and 04). The gallery therefore uses the three categories in the sitemap: Terrariums, Kokedama and Custom compositions. Format-based categories can be added when the catalogue structure is final.
- **Sections 03 and 04 are conditional.** Featured work and custom work are shown only if confirmed, usable photographs exist. If not, omit the section. Do not pad the page.
- **Gallery is not availability.** Completed work may include pieces that are no longer available. Captions never say "available", "in stock" or "sold".

**Decisions recommended for this page**

1. **One continuous gallery with category labels**, rather than separate pages per category. With a small number of images, separate pages would be thin.
2. **No captions in the grid.** Captions appear in the image viewer and under featured work. This keeps the grid minimal, as the blueprint asks, while still giving information.
3. **No filters until there are enough images in more than one category.** Show category labels only when each has enough images to justify it. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION: number of usable photographs]
4. **Consistent image ratios** where possible, so the grid looks composed rather than assembled.

### Image captions [TEMPLATE]

Captions appear in the image viewer and, where used, beneath featured images. They are short, factual and consistent.

**Full view of a terrarium**

- Line 1: {Piece name}
- Line 2: {Character line}, for example, "{Vessel_form} with {main hardscape element} and {main plant or moss}." (the character line pattern from document 04)

**Close-up**

- {Element}, detail. (Name only what is visible and confirmed, for example "Moss and root, detail.")

**Kokedama**

- Line 1: {Piece name}
- Line 2: {Plant_name} kokedama.

**Custom composition**

- Line 1: Custom composition
- Line 2: {One factual line about the vessel and main elements}

**Caption rules**

- 20 words or fewer per caption.
- Only elements that are visible and confirmed.
- No adjectives of praise. No "beautiful", "stunning", "exquisite" or "perfect".
- No "available", "in stock", "sold", prices or dimensions unless confirmed for display.
- No customer names, locations or occasions unless the customer has given permission.
- The first line has no full stop. The second line is a full sentence with a full stop.
- Terrarium captions never use kokedama vocabulary. Kokedama captions never use *vessel*, *glass*, *hardscape* or *landscape*.

### Alt text [TEMPLATE]

Describe what is visible and nothing else. This matches the pattern in document 04.

- **Terrarium, full view:** {Piece name}: {format} terrarium with {principal elements}.
- **Close-up:** {Piece name}: close view of {element}.
- **Kokedama:** {Piece name}: kokedama with {plant_name}.
- **Custom composition:** Custom composition: terrarium with {principal elements}.
- Where the piece has no confirmed name, begin with "Terrarium" or "Kokedama".

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Give the gallery its labels, captions and accessibility text, with minimal visible words.
- **Category labels:** "All", "Terrariums", "Kokedama" and "Custom compositions". The label "Custom compositions" matches the sitemap and the Collection page (document 03). The gallery descriptor for custom compositions is worded for completed work ("developed around a customer's requirements") and differs from the Collection descriptor ("discussed around your size, budget and setting"), which refers to future enquiries. The difference is deliberate.
- **Format sub-categories:** Not used. Micro, Jar, Bowl, Cube and Micro, Medium, Large are unresolved and must stay separate. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION: catalogue structure]
- **Piece links:** An image links to "View this piece" only if the piece is currently listed in the Collection and product pages exist. Otherwise it has no link. It never links to an enquiry for a piece that is not listed.
- **Descriptors:** The terrarium and kokedama descriptors match document 03. Do not reword them.
- **Accuracy:** Captions are generated only from the product data sheet (document 04, section 3). A caption must match the corresponding product page exactly in facts.
- **Photography standards:**
  - Show the complete terrarium, not only close-ups, and include close-ups of meaningful elements (moss, roots, plants, substrate layers, vessel details) where useful.
  - Use consistent lighting and image treatment, and keep the product recognisable.
  - Do not retouch an image so that it misrepresents the plants, materials or vessel.
  - Real photographs only.
- **Photographer credit:** Not documented. Add credit lines only if the business wants them. [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]
- **Layout:** Large images, consistent ratios where appropriate, a responsive grid, and an optional full-screen image viewer with keyboard controls. No decorative frames, badges or hover effects that obscure the image.

### Website copy

**Eyebrow:** Featured
**Main heading:** Featured work

**For each featured composition (maximum three):**

- Large photograph (full view), with close-up images if available
- Piece name: [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION]

**CTA:** None

### Internal notes

- **Purpose:** Give selected pieces more presence, with supporting detail.
- **Source of copy:** Use the same description as the product page. Do not write a separate description for the gallery. This keeps facts consistent and avoids a second set of claims.
- **Selection:** The business chooses the featured pieces. Choose pieces with complete confirmed data and strong photography. Variety of format and plant character is preferable to three near-identical pieces.
- **If no piece has confirmed data:** Omit the section, or reduce it to one image with a caption only.
- **Not used:** "Signature piece", "bestseller", "our favourite", "limited edition". None is confirmed.
- **Visual:** Larger images than the grid, in full view first. Real photography only.

## Flags raised by this document

1. **Cinematic images.** The five homepage background images are not confirmed as real photographs. Do not use them in the gallery unless they are.
2. **Categories.** The blueprint's Jar, Bowl and Cube categories are not used, because those formats are not confirmed.
3. **Custom work.** No completed custom work is documented. Section 04 is conditional on its existence and on customer consent.
4. **Availability note.** The wording ("Pieces shown may no longer be available") is a sensible safeguard, but it implies the business can speak to availability. It needs confirmation.
5. **Image rights.** The business should confirm that it owns, or has permission to use, every photograph.
